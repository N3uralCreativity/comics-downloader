Privacy policy and legal notice

Last updated: 23 November 2026, for version 5

This policy covers the Comics Downloader extension, the optional Comics Downloader Plus service (the account, the Cloud Library, settings sync, and the web library), and the support helper on the status page. The extension works without an account, and nothing is sent to Plus unless you sign in.

Without an account: nothing about you is sent to the Developer. Your settings stay in your browser, and the extension talks only to the sites you allow, plus a check for the Developer’s notices and, outside Firefox, an anonymous daily install count.

With Plus: your email, your devices, and your subscription. Synced settings and Cloud Library pages are encrypted in your browser before upload. The service keeps a protected copy of your encryption key so an email code is enough on a new device; it does not open your data.

Scope and controller

Comics Downloader is a browser extension published by Leo Péron under the name N3uralCreativity (the Developer, we, or us). Comics Downloader Plus is an optional paid service for that extension, with an account, a Cloud Library, encrypted settings sync, a web portal, and a web library. The Developer is the controller of the personal data described here. This policy also covers the support helper on the status page. It does not cover the manga sites the extension works on, their image servers, browser stores, Stripe, Link, Resend, Cloudflare, GitHub, or Telegram, which process data under their own policies.

Comics Downloader and Plus are independent projects and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of the sites the extension works on.

Without an account

Comics Downloader needs no account and sends nothing about you to the Developer. It has no analytics, no advertising and no fingerprinting, and it keeps no history of what you read or download.

Notices from the Developer

When you open the panel, the extension reads a short public file of notices from the status page, at most once every six hours, so the Developer can tell you about a broken site or an update you need. The request carries no identifier and nothing about your use; like any web page, GitHub Pages, which hosts the file, sees your IP address. The ids of notices you dismiss stay in your browser. You can turn notices off in Settings.

Install count

Once a day, the extension in Chrome, Edge, Opera and other Chromium browsers tells the Plus service that one more install of that browser was in use today. The request carries only the browser's name (chrome, edge or opera): no identifier, no account, nothing about what you read. The service adds one to that day's count for that browser and keeps nothing else, not even the request's IP address; like any web request, Cloudflare, which runs the service, sees the IP address in transit. In Firefox the extension sends nothing; its count comes from Mozilla's public add-on statistics.

On the sites you allow

The extension reads a site only after you allow it in its Sites tab. On an allowed site it reads series and chapter pages to list the chapters, and downloads the page images straight from the site's own servers, the same requests your browser makes when you read there. On most allowed sites it also adds its download buttons to series pages. On Comix it reads, from the chapter you have open, the page addresses the site's reader already shows, and changes nothing on the page.

Kept in your browser

Your settings and per-series presets, the notices you dismissed, and, until the browser closes, the download queue. Messages about downloads are kept in memory only. Saved files go to your Downloads folder; right after each save, the extension removes its entry from the browser's download list, and the file stays.

Plus account and sign-in

Plus stays off until you sign in, from the Plus tab in the extension or on the account page. The first sign-in in the extension asks your browser for permission to contact the Plus service (and, in Firefox, for the optional data consent described under Browser-store disclosures). Plus then processes:

Email address
Used for six-digit sign-in codes, account identification, a reminder before a trial ends, payment problems, security notices such as a new-device sign-in, deletion warnings, and, if you joined the waiting list while Plus was full, one message when a place opens. It is not added to a marketing list.
Waiting list
If Plus is full when you try to start it, your account joins a waiting list with the time you joined and the time you were told a place opened. The entry is deleted with your account.
Feedback you send
If you use Tell the developer on the account page, your message, the topic you picked, your plan state, and where you sent it from (extension version and browser name, or the website) are emailed to the Developer through Resend. Your email address is included only when you allow a reply. The Plus service does not store the message; it only counts messages per account to limit them to five a day.
Authentication records
Hashed sign-in codes, hashed session tokens, expiry times, and revocation state. Raw tokens are stored by the extension or web library, not in readable form in the service database.
Device records
A random device ID, a device name such as browser and platform, a public ECDH key, approval state, and last-seen time. Up to five extension devices can be active; web sessions do not count toward that limit. A device you sign in to with an email code can open your cloud data right away, and we email you when a new device signs in.
Account key
A copy of the key that encrypts your cloud data, itself encrypted with a separate server secret and bound to your account, so an email code is enough to open your data on any device (see Settings sync and your account key).
Entitlement records
Trial, active, grace, cancellation, expiry, and renewal dates, plus Stripe customer and subscription identifiers. We do not receive full payment card details.
Storage records
For the Cloud Library: item counts, stored bytes, page counts and sizes, which item sits in which folder, upload and trash state, revision numbers, and timestamps. These enforce storage limits and keep devices in step.
Operational records
Request IDs, error codes, API timing, ciphertext size, schema version, revision number, and timestamps used for security and reliability. Logs must not include email, tokens, title names, reading records, file names, or ciphertext.
Rate-limit records
A keyed hash of the requesting IP address and time-window counters, used to limit sign-in code requests and API abuse. Counters are deleted after two days and are not used for tracking or advertising.

Cloud Library

The Cloud Library stores chapters you choose to save, so you can read them on your other devices and in the web library. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on Also save downloads to the Cloud Library in the extension, or add files in the web library, and items are private to your account: there is no sharing or publishing feature.

What is encrypted

Before upload, the extension encrypts every page with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your account encryption key, and binds each page to your account and to that item so it cannot be swapped with another. Chapter, folder, and series names, page file names, a small preview image of the first page, and your reading position in each chapter are encrypted the same way. Encrypted pages are stored in Cloudflare R2 and encrypted metadata in Cloudflare D1.

What the service can see

Your account, whether each item is a chapter or a folder and which folder it sits in, the number and encrypted size of its pages, a SHA-256 digest of each encrypted page used to verify uploads, upload and trash state, and timestamps. To avoid saving the same chapter twice, the extension also sends an identifier computed from the chapter address with your encryption key; the service cannot turn it back into a title or address. Because the service holds a protected copy of your account key (see Settings sync and your account key), it could technically decrypt these items; it does not open them, except where the law requires.

Limits

Each account has a storage limit and an item limit, shown in the Cloud Library. Each encrypted page can be up to 8 MiB, and each chapter up to 1,000 pages.

On your devices

In the extension, a chapter's encrypted pages stay in memory only while they upload; an interrupted upload starts again with the next download. In the web library, uploads keep their encrypted pages in the browser's IndexedDB so they can resume. Your item list is cached, still encrypted, so the library opens offline. Pages you save for offline reading stay encrypted on the device until you open them, and you can remove them from a device at any time.

Settings sync and your account key

Settings sync is off until you turn it on in the extension's Plus tab. It covers your extension settings and per-series presets. The extension encrypts them in your browser with AES-256-GCM before upload. The service receives only the encrypted envelope and limited metadata: opaque account and device identifiers, revision, schema version, size, and timestamps, stored in Cloudflare R2 and D1.

Earlier versions of the extension could also sync watched series, reading progress, downloaded-chapter markers, and reading statistics. Version 5 does not use them; if an older version on another of your browsers still syncs them, they stay in your encrypted copy unchanged.

Your account key is created in your browser. The service keeps a copy of it, encrypted with a separate server secret and bound to your account, and hands it to a browser only after you sign in there with an email code. That is what lets an email code alone open your data on a new device, and it also means the service could technically decrypt your data. We do not open it, except where the law requires.

If your account's key was made before the service kept copies (before September 2026) and only an old browser has it, you can start over from the Plus tab: this deletes your synced settings and your Cloud Library, which nothing can read without that key, and makes a new key. Local extension data is not affected.

Sync never includes downloaded files, active downloads, cookies, or browser-check information. Chapter pages go to Plus only through the Cloud Library.

Copying reading lists

With Plus you can copy a reading list from one supported site to another, or from a MyAnimeList export file. The copy runs in your browser, through your own signed-in tabs of the two sites, using the same requests the sites' own buttons send: it reads your list on the first site, searches the second site for each series, and adds the series you confirm. A tab is opened in the background when none is open and closed afterwards. A copy only adds series; it never changes or removes what is already on a list.

Your lists are not sent to the Plus service and nothing is kept after the copy. A file you choose as a source is read in your browser and not uploaded. Your watched list from version 4, if you had one, is read from your own encrypted Plus data, in your browser.

MangaDex accepts list changes only with your MangaDex sign-in token, which mangadex.org keeps in your browser. When a copy involves MangaDex, the extension reads that token from your open MangaDex tab, keeps it in memory for that copy only, sends it only to MangaDex's API at api.mangadex.org, and stays under MangaDex's published rate limit. It is never stored or sent anywhere else.

When a series is not found on the target site under its title, and you allowed AniList when the copy started, the extension asks AniList's public API (graphql.anilist.co) for that series' other titles, sending only its AniList or MyAnimeList id, and searches the target under those too.

Web portal and web library

The Plus web portal handles sign-in, billing, and account settings. The web library lets you read your Cloud Library in any browser and can be installed as an app.

Cookie
The portal sets one strictly necessary cookie, cdl_plus_session, which holds your sign-in session. It is HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax, lasts up to 30 days, and is cleared when you sign out. Plus uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.
Browser storage
The web library keeps its sign-in session, device keys, and theme choice in the browser's local storage. It opens your library as soon as you sign in with an email code, like any other device.
Shared sign-in
When Comics Downloader in the same browser has been allowed to reach Plus, a small extension script runs on the portal and web library pages. It tells the page whether the extension is signed in and with which email, and passes single-use sign-in codes between them, so signing in or out on one side does the same on the other. The page keeps the time of its last sign-in or sign-out in local storage to tell which side acted last. The script reads no other page content and is never added for free users.
Offline use
A service worker caches the web library's own files so it opens without a connection. Pages you save offline stay encrypted in IndexedDB, as described under Cloud Library.

Billing and transactional email

Stripe Managed Payments provides checkout for the 30-day card-backed trial and the US$1.50 monthly subscription, with Link acting as merchant of record. Stripe and Link handle payment methods, applicable indirect taxes, invoices, renewals, refunds, transaction support, and legally required records. Plus receives only the identifiers and subscription state needed to grant access.

Stripe and Link deliver receipts and subscription messages. Resend delivers sign-in codes, new-device security alerts, the one-time seven-day trial reminder, and deletion warnings from plus@n3uralcreativity.top. Plus sends no newsletters, abandoned-cart campaigns, or promotional email.

A one-way email hash and Stripe customer ID may remain after account deletion solely to enforce one trial per verified account or customer and prevent repeated-trial abuse. This record is not used to contact, profile, or advertise to you.

Browser permissions

storage
Keeps your settings, the download queue until the browser closes, and, when you are signed in, your Plus sign-in and encryption key on this device.
downloads
Saves the finished CBZ, ZIP, or PDF files, then removes their entries from the browser's download list. The files stay.
scripting
Adds the download buttons only on the sites you allowed. On Comix only, reads the page addresses from the chapter you have open, without changing the page. With Plus, when you copy a reading list, sends the two sites' own list requests from your tabs of them.
declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess
Adds the Referer some image servers require, and removes the Origin header, on the extension's own image requests for allowed sites only. Pages you browse are never affected.
offscreen and sidePanel (Chrome, Edge, Opera)
The side panel is the extension's interface. A hidden extension page hands finished files to the download manager. Firefox uses its sidebar instead.
Each site you allow (optional)
Asked when you press Allow for that site, so the extension can read its pages and download its images. Nothing runs on a site you have not allowed.
Image servers on .site addresses (optional, Comix)
Comix serves chapter images from many changing .site addresses. Only the page addresses your open chapter already shows are fetched.
cookies (optional, MangaHub)
MangaHub's own reader sends its access cookie as a request header; the extension copies that one cookie the same way. No other cookie is read.
The Plus service (optional)
Asked the first time you sign in to Plus, and used only for the account, subscription status, settings sync, and the Cloud Library.

Retention and deletion

  • Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes, allow five attempts, and are deleted within a day of expiring. Codes that pass a sign-in between the website and the extension work once, expire after two minutes, are stored only as hashes, and are deleted within a day of expiring.
  • Access tokens expire after 15 minutes; rotating refresh sessions and the portal cookie expire after 30 days or on sign-out or revocation.
  • The latest encrypted sync state and one daily restore point are kept for 30 days. A limited number of Before restore points are also kept within that period.
  • Cloud Library items in the trash can be restored for 7 days, then they and their encrypted pages are permanently deleted. Deleting an item permanently from the trash removes it right away. Uploads left unfinished for 24 hours are removed.
  • Cancellation keeps Plus active through the paid or trial period. Failed payments receive a seven-day grace period.
  • Expired accounts are read-only for 90 days: you can still read, export, and delete your sync data and Cloud Library items, but not add new ones. After that, encrypted snapshots, sync metadata, and every Cloud Library item are deleted. We email a warning 7 days before.
  • Delete cloud data removes sync snapshots and leaves the account and Cloud Library in place. Starting over, for an account whose key only an old browser has, removes snapshots, the key, and every Cloud Library item, which nothing can read without that key. Both preserve local data.
  • Delete account cancels the subscription, revokes sessions and devices, and deletes synced data and every Cloud Library item. If the cancellation fails, nothing is deleted and you can try again. Stripe and Link retain records required by law; the one-trial anti-abuse record described under Billing and transactional email remains.
  • Local extension data stays in your browser until you remove it or uninstall the extension.

Security

Controls include HTTPS, local authenticated encryption for sync and Cloud Library data, encryption bound to each account and item, opaque random identifiers, hashed tokens and codes, short access-token lifetime, rotating refresh tokens, sign-in attempt limits, per-email, per-IP, and per-account rate limits, new-device email alerts, a server-side secret protecting stored account keys, webhook HMAC verification, idempotent webhook processing, five-device enforcement, encrypted restore points, and deletion controls.

The account key is stored in browser local storage so the extension and web library work without signing in again each time. Anyone who can read your email can sign in to your account, so keep your email account secure; anyone who fully controls your browser profile may also be able to access the key. No electronic system is absolutely secure.

Stripe Managed Payments provides checkout, and Link acts as merchant of record. Browser stores distribute packages. GitHub hosts this website, the status page, and its issue reports. Telegram carries live chats you start from the status page. These providers may process data in countries outside your own under their terms, data-processing agreements, and legally recognized transfer mechanisms.

We do not sell personal data, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness.

The status page and its helper

The status page at status.n3uralcreativity.top is hosted by GitHub Pages. Its support helper runs in your browser: nothing you type there is sent anywhere unless you open the GitHub report it writes, which you then file under your own GitHub account.

When the Developer is online, the helper can connect you to them. Only if you choose to, what you type and what you told the helper are sent through a relay on Cloudflare to the Developer's Telegram account. The chat uses a random identifier kept in that browser tab, with no account. The relay keeps no copy of the messages: it remembers which Telegram message belongs to which chat (the last 300), and, only while you are connected, a short hash of your IP address to limit chats per visitor. The conversation itself stays in the Developer's Telegram.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability. The extension's Plus tab and the account page provide account status, device removal, sign-out, and account deletion, and the Cloud Library lets you download your chapters. For any other request, email leoperon4@gmail.com from your verified account email, or include it, so the account can be located.

EU and UK processing bases include performance of the Plus contract for the account, Cloud Library, sync, and billing; consent for optional categories and permissions; legitimate interests for security, abuse prevention, and the status page's live chat; and legal obligations for billing records. You may complain to your local data-protection authority.

Browser-store disclosures

Without an account

Downloads need no account and no Plus permission, and nothing is sent to the Developer. Data is not used for advertising, unrelated profiling, lending, or sale.

When you sign in to Plus

Chromium browsers ask for permission to contact the Plus service. Firefox also asks for optional consent to process personally identifying information (your account email), authentication information (sign-in and sessions), browsing activity (a keyed identifier of each chapter you save to the Cloud Library, used to avoid saving it twice), website content (the chapter pages you save to the Cloud Library, encrypted before upload), and technical and interaction data (a device name such as "Firefox on Windows", and the minimal errors, sizes, and timings needed to run the service). Declining leaves downloads available.

Children

The extension is a general utility and is not directed to children. Plus requires the legal capacity to enter a paid subscription, or authorization from a parent or guardian where applicable. We do not knowingly create Plus accounts for children who cannot lawfully consent. Third-party comic content may be unsuitable for minors and remains the responsibility of the site and the user or guardian.
