Moving to another site? Copy your reading list there instead of adding every series again. Cloud Library

Set Save to to Cloud Library, or Computer and Cloud, next to the Download button in the extension’s panel, and each chapter you download goes to your library. Read it in the web library in any browser, phones included, or install the web library as an app; pages you pick stay available offline.

  • 5 GB per account. The library shows how much you use.
  • A chapter you already saved isn’t saved twice.
  • Items are private to your account; there is no sharing.
  • Deleted items stay in the trash for 7 days.

Settings sync

Your settings and per-series presets follow you to every browser you sign in on. Changes upload a few seconds after you make them, and other browsers pick them up when they start.

  • Restore points from the last 30 days put your settings back as they were.
  • Signing in takes a six-digit code from your email. There is no password.
  • You can see every signed-in browser and remove any of them.

What the server sees

Your email, your devices, your subscription, and how much you store. Not your pages, and not what you read.

In your browserPages, chapter and series names, and settings are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before upload.
On the serverOnly the encrypted data, and its size.
On your other devicesA code sent to your email is all a new browser needs to read it.

For that last part to work, the server keeps a copy of your encryption key, itself encrypted with a server secret. That copy means the server could technically read your data. It doesn’t, unless the law requires it. The details are in the privacy policy.

How to start

  1. Open Comics Downloader’s Plus tab and enter your email.
  2. Type the six-digit code from the email.
  3. Press Start the 30-day free trial and finish the checkout.
  4. Pick Cloud Library under Save to, turn on settings sync in the Plus tab, or open Copy reading lists.

If Plus ends

Your synced settings and your Cloud Library stay readable for 90 days, so you can read, download or delete them. Then they are deleted; you get an email a week before. Nothing on your computer is touched, and the extension keeps working.
