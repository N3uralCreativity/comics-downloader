The same thing, on a real site

Recorded on chikari.moe: open the panel, pick a chapter, download it. Nothing is sped up.

  1. Allow a site

    The extension does nothing on a site until you allow it in the Sites tab. One click, once per site.

  2. Pick your chapters

    Tick the ones you want, or press Download all. Several scanlation groups? You choose which.

  3. Read them anywhere

    One file per chapter lands in your Downloads folder, named and numbered for your reader app.

Buttons that look like they belong there

A series page gets Download all next to the site’s own buttons and a checkbox on every chapter. They take the site’s colours, corners and font, so nothing looks bolted on.

  • Tick a few chapters and a bar appears with the format and one Download button.
  • The buttons sit in a sealed box the site’s own code can’t read or change.
  • Chapters that cost coins on the site are left out.

A side panel for everything else

Click the toolbar icon and the panel opens beside the page. It lists the chapters of whatever series is in the tab, and it works on every supported site, even the few where buttons on the page aren’t possible.

  • Click a row, then Shift-click another, to pick everything in between.
  • Pause, resume or cancel a download, and retry only the chapters that failed.
  • Each site is paced on its own, so one slow site doesn’t hold up the rest.

Files your reader app already understands

Each chapter becomes a CBZ with the series, chapter number, group and date written inside, so Kavita, Komga, Mihon and Panels sort it without help. Pages keep the quality the site serves; nothing is re-compressed.

  • CBZ, ZIP or PDF
  • Several chapters in one ZIP
  • Kavita and Komga naming
  • Your own name patterns
  • Any folder inside Downloads

It keeps to itself

  • You choose the sites.It reads a site only after you allow that site from the panel.
  • No reading history.No history, no reading stats and no account. The queue is gone when the browser closes.
  • A clean download list.Saved chapters stay on your disk; their entries are cleared from the browser’s download list.
  • No tricks on browser checks.If a site asks for one, its downloads pause until you pass it in your own tab.
