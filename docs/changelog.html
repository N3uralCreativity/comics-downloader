What’s new in 5.0.0 Commit history

5.0.0

November 2026

A rebuild from scratch. Comics Downloader now works on dozens of manga sites instead of one, and the package is about 26 times smaller (132 KB instead of 3.4 MB).

New

  • Download buttons on each supported site’s own series pages: Download all next to the site’s buttons, and a checkbox on every chapter. They take the site’s colours and corners.
  • A side panel with the chapter list of the series in the current tab, the download queue, the settings and Plus.
  • 42 sites at launch. The status page checks them every 30 minutes and says why other sites are left out.
  • A support helper on the status page: it answers whether a site is working, walks through the usual fixes, can put you in touch with me when I’m online, and writes a report you can file on GitHub.
  • Chapters per file: one CBZ, ZIP or PDF per chapter, or several chapters in one ZIP. A Kavita and Komga naming option.
  • Each site is paced on its own. A site that asks for a browser check pauses only its own downloads until you pass it in your tab.
  • Plus: chapters you download can go to your Cloud Library, encrypted in your browser. Settings sync covers your settings and per-series presets, with restore points. Pick Cloud Library under Save to in the panel.
  • Short notices from me at the top of the panel when a site breaks or an update is needed. They can be turned off in Settings.

Changed

  • The extension reads a site only after you allow it in the Sites tab.
  • Comix works from the panel only, one chapter at a time: open the chapter, scroll to the end, then download it.
  • Saved files stay in Downloads; their entries are removed from the browser’s download list. Nothing about what you read is kept.
  • Plus: an email code is all a new browser needs. Recovery codes are gone; accounts with an old key can start over.
  • A new look and a blue icon.

Removed

  • Watched series and new-chapter checks, reading progress, reading stats, the Release Agenda and the “already downloaded” markers.
  • The right-click menu, notifications, community badges, and Mihon support.

Your settings and your Plus sign-in carry over from version 4. If another browser still runs version 4, its synced watched series and reading progress stay as they are.
