Comics Downloader saves manga chapters from the sites you already read as CBZ, ZIP or PDF files. It's no longer only for comix.to: it works on many sites, and it stays a browser extension.

If you already have Comix Downloader, you don't need to do anything: it updates to 5.0 on its own, and your settings and Plus sign-in carry over.

### What you get

- **Buttons on the site itself**: Download all next to the site's own buttons, and a checkbox on each chapter.
- **A side panel** with the chapter list, the download queue and the settings, on every supported site.
- **CBZ with ComicInfo.xml** for Kavita, Komga and other readers, plain ZIP, or PDF.
- **Live status** of every site at [status.n3uralcreativity.top](https://status.n3uralcreativity.top), with a 24/7 support helper.
- **Plus**, optional: a Cloud Library, your settings on every browser, and copying your reading lists from one site to another. [More on the site](https://n3uralcreativity.top/comics-downloader/plus.html).

### What changed since 4.3.6

- Watched series, reading progress, stats and the Release Agenda are gone. Your other v4 browsers keep their copies.
- On Comix, downloads work one chapter at a time from the panel, after you scroll through the chapter.
- About 26 times smaller (132 KB instead of 3.4 MB), and faster.

The full list is in the [changelog](https://n3uralcreativity.top/comics-downloader/changelog.html).

### Something not working?

- Check the site on the [status page](https://status.n3uralcreativity.top) and ask its helper.
- [Open an issue](https://github.com/N3uralCreativity/comics-status/issues/new?template=site-problem.yml) and say which site, which chapter and what you saw.
- **Missing a site?** Suggest it on the [announcement thread](https://github.com/N3uralCreativity/comics-downloader/issues/41).
